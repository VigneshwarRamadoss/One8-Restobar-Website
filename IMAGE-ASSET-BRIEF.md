## Antigravity instruction

Use the three supplied images as visual references for crop, light, texture and pacing. Preserve One 8's paper, ink, mineral, sand and restrained terracotta palette. Do not recolour them with heavy orange-and-teal grading, place text inside them, add fake logos/signage, or infer venue facts from their content. Use `next/image`, explicit dimensions, responsive `sizes`, AVIF/WebP delivery and focal-position controls. Replace all three with approved real photography before production launch when the client asset library becomes available.
